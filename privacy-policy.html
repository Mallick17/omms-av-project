Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or our website contact form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address (if provided), city, event details, and any other information you voluntarily share with us for the purpose of processing your rental enquiry.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to respond to your rental enquiries, confirm bookings, provide customer support, and send relevant updates about your order. We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your information may be shared internally across our city teams (Hyderabad, Bangalore, Mumbai, Chennai, Pune) only to process your booking efficiently.

4. WhatsApp Communications

When you click our WhatsApp links, you are directed to WhatsApp's platform. Any communication through WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We use WhatsApp solely for booking communication and customer support.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted through digital channels.

6. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at contact@ommsaudiovisuals.com or via WhatsApp at +91 70082 86477.
